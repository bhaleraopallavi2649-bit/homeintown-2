A separate journey for booked customers

After a booking, send relevant, factual project updates as part of the customer service journey. Template category depends on the message purpose and current WhatsApp policy—a booking alone does not automatically make promotional content a utility message.

  • Share progress at a useful cadence and only when there is a meaningful update.
  • Keep project milestones, timelines and changes accurate and clearly explained.
  • Make support and contact details easy to find in every customer communication.

A practical checklist for preparing WhatsApp message templates. Confirm current Meta requirements in WhatsApp Manager before submitting.

Image headers for project updates

Use a clear, relevant project image to make the update easy to recognise. Choose an authentic photo and do not present illustrative imagery as a current site photo.

  • Choose an IMAGE header when supported; provide a compliant sample image during submission.
  • For supported media variables, attach the current image when sending instead of creating a new template for each photo.
  • Label dated progress photos accurately and never present an old image as current.
  • Check the latest WhatsApp image dimensions and file-size requirements before upload.

Dynamic links and lead tracking

Use an approved dynamic URL button to direct each recipient to the relevant project page. Keep tracking tokens opaque, unique and limited to consented analytics.

Choose the correct template category

Message purposeTypical categoryImportant note
Property discovery or promotional offerMARKETINGUse for promotional or lead-nurture content.
Requested booking, service or account updateUTILITYMust be specific, expected and non-promotional.
  • Submit separate templates when the audience, purpose or content category differs.
  • Keep utility messages focused on the customer's specific transaction; do not add promotional calls to action.
  • Obtain appropriate opt-in and provide a clear way to stop marketing messages.

Frequency, consent and quality checks

  • Set a conservative frequency cap and send only to people who have opted in.
  • Monitor blocks, opt-outs, delivery and template quality; pause and investigate unusual changes.
  • Stop marketing outreach promptly when someone opts out or withdraws consent.
  • Review WhatsApp Manager and applicable policy updates regularly.
Do not rely on fixed rate limits or category assumptions in this guide. WhatsApp policies and template requirements can change—verify them before sending.
